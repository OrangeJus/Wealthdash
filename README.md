# 📊 Local Personal Wealth & Finance Dashboard

A privacy-first, local web application designed to give you absolute control over your personal finance, savings targets, and investment portfolios. Running completely on `localhost`, this app ensures your sensitive financial data remains strictly yours.

## ✨ Core Features

*   **🏠 Comprehensive Dashboard Layout:** Monitor your Total Balance, Monthly Income/Expenses, and Net Worth inside a single, clean, and scannable viewport.
*   **🛑 Dual Progress Bars (The Brake & The Gas):** Visualizes your financial discipline at a glance using an *Expense Limit Bar* (to hold back spending) and a *Savings Target Bar* (to push your wealth goals).
*   **⇄ Multi-Wallet & Intelligent Transfers:** Track money movement between cash, bank accounts, and e-wallets seamlessly without messing up your actual income/expense analytics.
*   **🔄 Smart Savings with Rollover:** Set monthly savings goals with an automated deficit accumulation system—if you miss this month's target, the gap automatically rolls over to the next month.
*   **📈 Integrated Stock Portfolio & RDN Tracker:** A streamlined investment module with RDN wallet integration, manual market price adjustments, and a smart unified "Sell" action that automatically calculates realized P&L and credits back your RDN.
*   **📊 Deep-Dive Analytics:** Historic cash flow charts, category spending rankings, and real-time asset allocation donut charts (`Liquid Cash` vs `Savings` vs `Investments`).
